| Honest screenshot evidence | `aso-store-listing/SKILL.md` is a store-presentation reference; actual runtime/source provenance was used rather than claiming the target PNGs are app screenshots | Gallery explicitly separates target/before/after; native build and exact visual-match limits are stated |

The other supplied audio, compliance, FTUE, ads, live-ops, telemetry, social and world-generation standards were reviewed as existing-system context. This pass does not introduce audio packs, policy claims, monetization, live-ops calendars, tracking, server rankings or new worlds. Icon-only help text was adjusted to refer to icons rather than removed button captions. No external “current trend” assertion or new policy research was used to fill missing reference content.

`player-movement-platforming` and `sprite-animation-pipeline` are not files in the supplied16-document set. The crouch finding comes from the ten actual manifest entries, the supplied PNG sheets and `scripts/animate-assets.py`; it is not a quotation from those absent documents.
